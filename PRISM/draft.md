Hi, I’m Mingjia Huo, a PhD student from UC San Diego. Our project generates third-person game videos that respond to user actions.
The challenge is maintaining both visual fidelity and control, as errors accumulate over time.
We train on simulated data and annotated videos, using three stages to enable thirty-second streaming rollouts.
Our model improves visual quality and action following compared with existing baselines. Training across multiple domains also improves generalization to unseen environments.
To scale to minute-long, high-resolution generation, we need new hardware architectures to handle the memory and data movement demands of the key-value cache.
